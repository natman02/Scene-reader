SCENE READER

Reads the other parts aloud and comes in on cue when you finish your line.

Use NAME: line on each line, or standard screenplay layout (name in capitals on its own line, dialogue underneath). Anything in (brackets) is shown but not spoken.

Reader voices

Pick the most natural-sounding voice for each character.

Timing & sensitivity

Raise this if it jumps in during your dramatic pauses.

Casting likes the reader quieter than you.

On iPad: keep Scene Reader on screen while you film on another device. For better voices, download an "Enhanced" English voice in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.

Keys: Space or a clicker's next button = next cue / skip. Esc = stop. Click any line to start from there.
